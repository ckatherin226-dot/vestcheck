# Annotation visual review — Phase 2.2B

Date: **2026-11-05**. Reviewer: **Codex image inspection using image-viewing tools**. This is actual assistant visual inspection, **not independent human expert adjudication** and not a full-dataset annotation-accuracy study. No review result was inferred from a filename or merely generated by a script.

## Sampling and views

Twenty unique images cover **five target classes × two publisher splits × two filename families** (`source-` and `ultralytics-ppe-nov2026-`). Filename families are not verified real/generated labels. For each stratum, sort candidates by their smallest box area for that source class and then path; choose the lower quartile for `source-` and median for the other family, excluding already selected images. The deterministic algorithm is in `model/review_dataset.py`.

All 20 annotated overviews were opened. For the ten source-family images, enlarged nearest-neighbor context crops were also opened: the smallest target region plus a containing person, where present. Enlargement exposes original pixels and does not add visual information. All target classes are overlaid, with person/helmet/vest/no-helmet/no-vest labels and source coordinate transforms. The local `review_sample.json`, PNG diagnostics and scene-feature cache remain ignored; source hashes and observed notes are preserved in [the committed review record](../model/reviews/phase_2_2b_review.json).

## Interpretation and limits

- Ten examples had broadly compatible visible regions in this inspection; four require small-target/context review and six have limited visibility. These categories describe the selected views, not measured label precision, error rates, accepted training labels or generalization.
- Ambiguous negatives include a forklift-cab operator (S03), backlit head (S13), tiny partially occluded subject near a truck (S15), low-light torso (S17), and distant pixelated torso (S19). No-helmet/no-vest labels in such cases must not be cleared solely because a detector or overlay says they are present.
- All five target classes were seen. Person/PPE boxes alone do not encode ownership: crowds, crouched postures, reflected subjects, vehicle occlusion and neighboring PPE need a separate relation/visibility rubric. Correct wearing, required PPE and regulatory compliance were not assessed.
- The review identified uncertainty, not a correction ground truth. No semantic relabeling, deletion, or uncertainty reassignment was applied. Only the separately documented numerical edge clipping was made in derived target labels.
- Have a second qualified reviewer adjudicate uncertain examples and a wider representative sample, documenting region visibility, ordinary clothing versus high-visibility garments, reflective duplicates, carried/unworn PPE and ownership. Do not turn occlusion or absent annotation into a missing-PPE label.
- A separate 42-image scene-search inspection is recorded in the JSON and [split policy](SPLIT_POLICY.md). There is no worker identification or cross-session identity matching.

The visual review was performed; no image-viewing blocker remained. Remaining blockers concern **insufficient source detail, missing relational/provenance annotations and lack of independent expert review**, rather than an unavailable viewing tool.
